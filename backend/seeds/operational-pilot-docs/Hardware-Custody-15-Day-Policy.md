# Corporate Policy: Hardware Custody & 15-Day Temporary Loan Rule
**Document ID:** POL-OPS-2026-001  
**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Applicability:** All Blue Hawk Technologies Employees, Field Technicians, and Contractors  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Review Frequency:** Semiannual (Every 6 months)  
**Authority:** POLITICS.md Section 2.1 & 2.2  
**Custodian:** Johan Vasquez  

---

### 1. 15-Day Maximum Loan Period
1. No collaborator, technician, or developer may retain on temporary loan, field trial, or remote work any hardware asset (laptops, testing consoles, mobile terminals, lab switches) for more than **fifteen (15) continuous calendar days**.
2. Upon reaching day 15, the custodian must physically present the equipment to **Johan Vasquez** at the central warehouse for functional verification and system audit.
3. Extensions require prior written approval from the **IT Operations Directorate** or **Lead Systems Custodian** before the expiration date.

### 2. Physical Cleanliness & Preservation
- Eating, liquids, coffee, grease, or unauthorized disassembly of company equipment is strictly prohibited.
- Assets must be returned in pristine physical cleanliness. Negligence charges apply for physical damage.

### 3. QR Labeling & Tracking
- Every asset must bear its thermal adhesive QR label `BH-YYYY-XXXX`. Removing or transferring labels is strictly prohibited.
